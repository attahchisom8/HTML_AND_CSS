Expense Tracker

Welcome to Mastermose Tech

1. Log Your Spending:
Use the form at the top to enter your expense name, category, and price. Click "Add" to save it.

2. Change Your View:
Use the tabs to switch between your spending for this week, the last 3 weeks, or your entire history.

3. Watch the Charts:
Your spending habits are shown in the graphs. If a category turns red, it means you've spent 3x more than your usual average there!

4. Edit or Delete:
Use the icons next to each expense in the table to fix mistakes or remove old items.
